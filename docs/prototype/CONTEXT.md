# Context — JPA Operations Workspace Prototype

## Why this exists

JPA handles school procurement orders. One operator may simultaneously manage many schools whose orders are at different points: sales/intake, ARKAS collection, HET checking, SIPLah purchase, vendor preparation, goods arrival, delivery, school payment, benefit payment, and completion.

The current problem is not merely lack of visibility. The operator repeatedly has to remember what has happened, copy the same order data between documents, calculate/check totals, consolidate multiple school orders for the vendor, follow up payments, and remember which next action belongs to which school.

The prototype must explore whether software can reduce that cognitive and administrative load.

## Real-world workflow

1. A school agrees to order books/goods from JPA.
2. The school sends ARKAS, or JPA photographs/scans ARKAS at the school.
3. Order lines are checked against the JPA HET master to determine whether ARKAS totals match HET and where differences exist.
4. JPA obtains access to the school's SIPLah account. Do not store the school's real SIPLah password in this prototype.
5. JPA uses the school account to buy the requested items from JPA's store on TokoLadang/SIPLah.
6. SIPLah generates a Surat Pesanan. JPA sends that document to the school as proof the purchase was created.
7. Orders from multiple schools that are ready are consolidated into one vendor recap/batch so the book supplier can prepare the goods.
8. Vendor goods normally arrive at JPA's location, already separated/prepared per school.
9. JPA checks goods before sending them to each school. Good items may be delivered even if some items are still missing/replacement is pending.
10. Detailed school-delivery completeness is managed by a separate Kelengkapan Buku Tracker. This workspace only consumes a fulfillment summary.
11. School payment timing is independent of physical fulfillment. A school may pay before or after goods are complete.
12. After the school's full payment is verified, a school benefit becomes eligible.
13. Benefit is paid once in full at 10% of the final invoice/ARKAS amount paid by the school, not 10% of net settlement after platform/tax deductions.
14. A company-level order can be closed when goods are fully received/accepted by the school, SIPLah/admin is complete, school payment is LUNAS, and benefit is PAID. Supplier liability can remain outstanding and does not block order closure.

## Core terms

### School
A customer institution. A school can have multiple orders over time.

### School Order
The central unit of work for one school's procurement transaction. It has its own internal order ID and may also carry SIPLah order number and ARKAS/activity references.

### ARKAS
The school's budgeting/procurement source document containing requested items, quantities, and budgeted prices/amounts. It can arrive as PDF, photo, scan, or manual entry.

### HET
JPA's product/HET master used to verify titles/products, quantities, prices, and total against ARKAS.

### HET Exception
An item where the ARKAS line does not confidently match the HET master, price differs, product is ambiguous, or manual review is required.

### SIPLah Process
Operational checklist for obtaining access, placing the order in JPA's TokoLadang/SIPLah store, recording the order number, obtaining the Surat Pesanan, and sending it to the school.

### Surat Pesanan
Order document generated by SIPLah after the school order is placed. It should be stored/attached and tracked as sent to the school.

### Vendor Batch
A cross-school procurement batch. Multiple eligible school orders are combined into one vendor recap. The batch must preserve both aggregated quantities by product and the school-by-school breakdown.

### Goods Arrival
A vendor batch/order milestone recording that vendor goods have arrived at JPA's location. Creating or generating a vendor batch is not the same as sending it to the vendor or receiving goods.

### Kelengkapan Buku Tracker
Separate source-of-truth app for detailed delivery/fulfillment. This workspace consumes a summary such as ordered qty, delivered qty, remaining qty, progress %, problem count, and last sync.

### School Payment
Money received from the school. Normal flow is one full payment, though timing is independent of delivery. Only the owner/operator confirms LUNAS. Evidence is required in the future production system.

### School Benefit
Cash/transfer benefit to the school/authorized recipient. It becomes ELIGIBLE only after school payment is fully LUNAS. Amount is exactly 10% of the final invoice/ARKAS amount. It is paid once in full.

### Supplier Payment
Payments to the book supplier/vendor can happen in installments. Supplier payment status is independent from school payment and order closure.

### Next Action
The single most useful actionable recommendation for an active order. It should usually be derived from current domain state, but the operator may override or snooze it.

### Timeline
Automatic operational history plus manual notes used for context recovery and auditability.

## Important state independence

Do not model the entire system as one giant linear status. The following can be independently true at the same time:

- lifecycle stage
- HET review status
- SIPLah status
- vendor batch status
- fulfillment progress
- school payment status
- benefit status
- supplier payment status

Example of a valid order state:

- Stage: DISTRIBUTION
- Fulfillment: 74%
- School payment: LUNAS
- Benefit: PAID
- SIPLah: COMPLETED
- Supplier payment: PARTIAL

## Prototype mindset

This repository is for product R&D. The question is not whether the UI looks like a polished SaaS dashboard. The question is whether the prototype can reduce repetitive work, reduce memory burden, surface exceptions, and make the next operational action obvious.
